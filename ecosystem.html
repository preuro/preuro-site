The Preuro Ecosystem

The Preuro ecosystem is designed to be a diverse and competitive monetary environment, composed of 10 digital assets in total. It is built upon one central token, $PREU, and a series of nine historic European currencies that will be launched over time.

The Central Token: $PREU

$PREU is the core of our ecosystem. Its fixed, immutable supply makes it a reliable store of value. It also functions as a utility and governance token, granting holders the right to vote on future developments and receive airdrops of newly launched historic currencies.

The 9 Historic Currencies

These are the currencies of Europe's great nations, reborn for the digital age. They will be launched sequentially, as decided by community vote.

  • German Mark (Germany)
  • Italian Lira (Italy)
  • Portuguese Escudo (Portugal)
  • French Franc (France)
  • Spanish Peseta (Spain)
  • Dutch Guilder (Netherlands)
  • Greek Drachma (Greece)
  • Austrian Schilling (Austria)
  • Irish Punt (Ireland)
