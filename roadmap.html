PREURO OFFICIAL ROADMAP

Phase 1: Foundation & Concept

Official Whitepaper release, core team formation, and finalization of the Preuro economic model.

Phase 2: Core Development

Development of smart contracts, building the initial testnet, and undergoing security audits.

Phase 3: Network Launch

Public launch of the Preuro mainnet, Token Generation Event (TGE), and initial exchange listings.

Phase 4: Ecosystem Growth

Launch of the first DApps, establishing strategic partnerships, and starting the community grants program.

Phase 5: Cross-Chain Expansion

Development of bridges to other major blockchains, enhancing interoperability and liquidity.

Phase 6: Decentralized Governance

Implementation of the Preuro DAO for community-led governance and future protocol updates.
